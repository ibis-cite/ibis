Find references. Copy citation keys.

A small shortcut between your reference library and your writing. Search Zotero or a BibTeX file, copy a citation key, and paste it into your draft.

Early preview. Ibis is still taking shape. Expect bugs and changing features while I work towards a version that is ready to share widely.

What is a citation key?

A short label for a reference, such as rivera2025. Your writing tool uses it to find the full reference in your library.

Here it stands for the fictional article “A place for reading notes” by A. Rivera (2025), in Example Journal.

Pandoc
[@rivera2025]
LaTeX
\cite{rivera2025}
Typst
@rivera2025

Your library. Your writing. Fewer interruptions.

  1. Open Ibis. Choose a Zotero library or a .bib file during setup.
  2. Find a reference. Type a few words from its author, title or year.
  3. Copy, then paste. Press Enter to copy; paste it where you write.

Prefer a formatted citation? Ibis can also copy one in APA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE or MLA. It does not insert directly into other apps or generate your document’s bibliography.

Zotero
Personal and chosen group libraries. Keep Zotero open and enable its local API once in settings.
BibTeX and BibLaTeX
Choose a .bib file, including an export kept up to date by Better BibTeX.
Windows, macOS and Linux
Use the app launcher or a keyboard shortcut. On Wayland, use the launcher for now.

The app reads your library without changing it. Searching and copying work offline after setup; update checks are opt-in. App privacy details on GitHub.

A small start, with room to grow

I’m building Ibis to make finding a reference feel quick and ordinary, wherever you write. The aim is a fast, open-source tool that works across platforms and fits around your library.

Now
Make the preview easier to set up, more accessible and more reliable. Fix the rough edges before sharing it widely.
Next
More citation styles, richer citation previews and access to Zotero libraries while Zotero is closed.
Further ahead
Direct insertion into other apps and more ways to connect your tools. These are plans, not features available today.

There are no promised dates. Follow the full roadmap on GitHub.
